Knowledge Virus Studio

Stories that stick.

Knowledge Virus is a small independent studio making documentary videos about history, science and unsolved mysteries: well-researched, clearly sourced, and honest about where facts end and speculation begins.

Our channels

  • The Almost Century — science and history "what if" scenarios.
  • Last Seen Ledger — unsolved mysteries, disappearances and cold cases, told with care.
  • Odd Minute — strange history and mysteries in under a minute.

About our production app

The studio runs a private, single-user production app called Knowledge Virus n8n. It is used only by the studio itself, to:

  • keep our production schedule and research notes in our own Google Sheets and Google Drive;
  • read view counts, audience statistics and public comments for our own YouTube channels, so we can see what viewers want next.

It is not offered to the public, and it never accesses anyone else's Google account. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses and how that data is handled.
