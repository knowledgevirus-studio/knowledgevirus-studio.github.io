Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy covers Knowledge Virus n8n, the private production app run by Knowledge Virus Studio ("we"). The app has a single user: the studio's own Google account. It does not let anyone else sign in, and it does not access other people's Google accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Google Sheets and Google Drive: spreadsheets and files that the studio created, used as its production schedule, research notes and file storage.
  • YouTube (read-only): statistics for the studio's own channels (views, watch time, audience and revenue reports), and public comments posted on the studio's own videos.

How the data is used

  • To plan and track the studio's videos.
  • To produce weekly performance summaries for the studio.
  • To find topics viewers are asking for in comments. Comment text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be summarised. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models.

Where it is stored and who sees it

  • Data is stored in the studio's own Google account and on the studio's own computer.
  • It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising.
  • It is not transferred to anyone else except as described above (Anthropic, for summarising) or where the law requires it.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled in line with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

The studio can revoke the app's access at any time in its Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored statistics and comment summaries are deleted when they are no longer needed for production.

Viewers

If you comment on one of our videos, your comment is public on YouTube. We only read it through YouTube's normal public features and the read-only access above. We do not collect any other information about viewers. To ask about or remove anything we hold, email us.
